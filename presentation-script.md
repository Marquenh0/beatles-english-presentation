## Slide 2 — The Four Beatles

The best-known Beatles lineup had four members. John Lennon was a singer, guitarist, and songwriter, and one of the band’s main founders. Paul McCartney played bass, sang, and wrote songs; his melodies became an important part of the group’s sound. George Harrison played guitar and wrote songs, including music influenced by Indian traditions. Ringo Starr played drums and also sang. His distinctive drumming helped give the band its identity. Together, these different contributions made the Beatles sound like a group rather than four musicians working separately.

## Slide 3 — The Beginning

The Beatles formed in Liverpool in 1960. Before they became famous, they played smaller shows in Liverpool and Hamburg, Germany. Those early club performances gave them experience and helped them grow as a band. In 1962, they released *Love Me Do*. The song marked the beginning of their professional success. From these early stages, the band moved quickly toward a much larger audience. Their story began in local music venues, but it would soon reach far beyond Liverpool.

## Slide 4 — Beatlemania

As the Beatles became more popular, especially among young people, the excitement around them grew into a phenomenon known as Beatlemania. Fans filled performances and showed intense enthusiasm for the band. In 1964, the Beatles arrived in the United States, expanding their popularity around the world. Their success was visible in music, newspapers, and youth culture. This moment shows how a band that began in Liverpool became an international event, with fans in many places following their music and style.

## Slide 5 — A Musical Revolution

The Beatles’ music changed over time. They began with simpler, energetic songs, then explored more experimental and psychedelic sounds. They tried new instruments, recording techniques, and musical styles. Their album *Sgt. Pepper’s Lonely Hearts Club Band* showed how creative the group could be in the studio. The band also helped people think of an album as a complete artistic work, rather than only a collection of separate songs. Their approach opened new possibilities for rock and pop music.

## Slide 6 — Cultural Legacy

The Beatles influenced more than music. Their hairstyles, clothing, and attitudes became references for young people in the 1960s. Other artists also learned from the band’s songwriting, album-making, and willingness to experiment with sound. Their work helped change expectations in the music industry. The Beatles entered the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1988, recognizing their historical importance. More than five decades after the band ended, their songs are still heard, studied, and reinterpreted by artists in different countries.

## Slide 7 — The End of an Era

Success did not prevent personal and creative differences from growing between the members. In 1970, the Beatles officially came to an end, and each musician continued with an individual career. The breakup closed one chapter, but it did not erase what they had made together. Their music continued to reach new generations. In a way, the contrast is striking: the band itself lasted for a limited period, while its shared work remained present long after the group separated.

## Slide 8 — *Yesterday* (2019)

The 2019 film *Yesterday* is a musical comedy and romance directed by Danny Boyle and written by Richard Curtis. Its main cast includes Himesh Patel, Lily James, Kate McKinnon, and Ed Sheeran. The story follows Jack Malik, a musician who has trouble finding success. After a mysterious worldwide blackout and an accident, he wakes up in a world where the Beatles never existed. Jack is the only person who remembers their songs, and that discovery changes his life.

## Slide 9 — A World Without The Beatles

Jack begins performing Beatles songs as if they were his own, and he gains fame. But the film also brings him into conflict with his own sense of honesty and with Ellie, his best friend, who has always believed in him. The alternate world makes the Beatles’ importance easier to imagine: if their songs had never existed, musical history might have developed differently. The film connects that idea to artistic recognition, honesty, friendship, and the personal meaning of the things we value.

## Slide 10 — Conclusion

The Beatles were much more than a rock band. Four young musicians from Liverpool helped transform music and culture, influenced artists, and reached people across borders and generations. Their group ended in 1970, but their songs continued to connect listeners. *Yesterday* makes that influence visible by showing a world that has lost the Beatles’ music. The film reminds us that music can shape history and personal lives. The Beatles did not just become part of music history. They helped write it.
